# Giving Life to All Things

Personified Project is an interaction platform for next-generation personified intelligence, dedicated to moving AI from a tool to a companion—a digital friend capable of long-term companionship and growing together with you.

We continuously explore memory, autonomy, long-term interaction, and depth of life, hoping that every conversation is not merely a question-and-answer exchange but a continuation of time.

We are currently working on life simulation, natural interaction, efficient computing, long-context and memory modeling, and related areas. If this is helpful to you, you are welcome to follow us, visit our [official website](https://moon.chumenta.cn), or [email us](mailto:us@chumenta.cn).

We will continue to provide updates on our work and remain committed to open source.
